## Discrepancies with section 7

1. Anthropic: the quote "Offline evals are question-and-answer pairs." is not on the page. The page says "offline evals, which are simple question / answer pairs."
2. Snowflake 2025: "392 questions" is not stated; it is the sum of the table's per-subset counts (60, 80, 111, 141). No Figures row; use the four cells.
3. Snowflake 2025: "21 percentage points" is the table's Improvement cell "21%"; the text says "approximately 20%". The Figures row uses the cell and notes the text.
4. Snowflake: the quote "achieves more than 90%+ SQL accuracy on real-world use cases" is from the behind-the-scenes post; the 2024 accuracy post says "to achieve an impressive 90%+ SQL accuracy on real-world use cases". The "close to 2x" wording is the behind-the-scenes post; the 2024 post says "nearly 2x as accurate".
5. Snowflake behind the scenes: "another text-to-SQL product" is "another text-to-SQL solution" on the page.
6. GitHub: "3x" is written "three times faster at returning the right answer".
7. WisdomAI: the page gives 20%, 50% and 85% exactly; section 7's "about" is not on the page.
8. WisdomAI: "LLM- and human-validated ground truth", marked a paraphrase in section 7, is verbatim on the page.
9. LinkedIn blog: the page's apostrophe in "we’ve" is curly; section 7 has a straight one.
10. Anthropic: the page names "the grader" without saying it is an LLM, and states "there’s often only a single correct answer using a single correct source". Neither contradicts section 7, but Anthropic is not counted as using an LLM judge (C-5).

No qualifying entry lost its basis. Grab remains the softest (a general model-evaluation harness, not a data analyst's answers), as section 7 already says.
