## What the readings show

**1 · The two engines do not share sources.** Across queries 1–3, the six AI
readings (three per engine) produced **zero shared domains** between Perplexity
and ChatGPT.
Perplexity went to vendor blogs; ChatGPT went to institutions and benchmarks.

**2 · But the engine is not the variable — the question is.** On query 4
Perplexity abandoned analytics vendors for Slack's blog, and ChatGPT abandoned
institutions for vendor docs. On query 5 ChatGPT went to vendor *documentation*.
Four distinct source-class behaviours in five queries.
**A citation strategy cannot be built per engine. It has to be built per
question type.** That changes the measurement plan: "AI citations" as a single
number would hide this entirely.

**3 · The persistent domains.** owox.com appears in four readings. cube.dev,
count.co and basedash each win more than one surface. None is a name in piece
B's census of 13.

**4 · Weak incumbents are real, and confirmed by observation.** On query 2,
page one is generic ML-model content that does not mention analysts. On query 4,
the top results are two to four years old. Both were scored "weak incumbent" in
the bank by inference; this reading confirms it.

**5 · The engines reach our conclusions without us.** ChatGPT independently
produced piece A's finding twice and piece F's method twice. Perplexity produced
six of F's eight steps. **The ideas are not ours alone; the evidence is.** None
of these answers rests on a run anyone did.

**6 · The problem phrasing is unserved.** Query 4 describes exactly what Dot
solves, and the category is not outranked there — it is outside the frame.
Dot's content is all solution-side. Nothing connects "my team is drowning in
requests" to "self-service analytics".
